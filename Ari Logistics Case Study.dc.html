01 — Overview

A courier that had outgrown its own website.

Ari Logistics moves parcels and freight along one of the Caribbean's busiest import lanes — dual warehouses in Miami and Shenzhen, consolidation, customs brokerage, and last-mile delivery across Jamaica.

The operation was sophisticated. The website was a brochure. The brief was to build something genuinely high fidelity that carries every piece of information the business needs to communicate — and makes the company feel as considered as the logistics behind it.

05 — The marketing site

Eleven sections, each answering the next question.

The homepage is sequenced like a conversation — capability, then proof, then process, then reach, then reassurance, then the ask.

SERVICES Eight offerings on a single fluid grid — cards lift into a blue glow on hover.
NETWORK USA and China landmasses sit behind animated routes; hovering a hub reveals warehouse, transit time and speed.
OPERATIONS Real photography of port, warehouse and doorstep — the trust argument the old site never made.

The whole site exists to make this moment feel inevitable.

06 — Rate calculator

Turning a price sheet into an answer.

The client supplied a ten-row rate table. Reading it, the model was obvious — a fixed first pound, a flat rate per additional pound, 15% GCT on top. Encoding the formula instead of the rows let the calculator reach 100 lbs without a line of extra data.

07 — Tracking

One field for three carriers.

Parcels arrive via DHL, FedEx and UPS, so customers were leaving to google carrier sites. The tracking page auto-detects the carrier from the number format and redirects straight to the right page — keeping Ari Logistics in the journey.

ep.shiptojm.com/track
08 — Responsive

Most of this traffic arrives on a phone.

Every grid is fluid rather than breakpoint-locked, so layouts reflow continuously instead of snapping. These are live frames — the real pages running at 390px.

09 — Interaction & motion

Motion that reports, never performs.

Every animation is tied to something real — a route being travelled, a figure being counted, an element entering view. Nothing loops purely for decoration in the reading path.

10 — Tools & build

Designed directly in code.

No framework, no build step, no component library. Three hand-authored pages that deploy as static files — fast, cheap to host, and trivial to hand to any future developer.
